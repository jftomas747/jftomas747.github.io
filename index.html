About this portfolio

Engineering documented as a process, not just a final result.

These linked case studies bring together project work from Cal Poly laboratories and engineering courses. Each site traces the path from problem definition through design decisions, fabrication or implementation, testing, and final outcomes.

The common thread is systems thinking: understanding how mechanical hardware, materials, sensors, controls, software, and operators interact—and turning that understanding into working prototypes.
